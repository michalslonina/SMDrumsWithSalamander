## This is a collection, not an adaptation

The two origins are combined side by side, not mixed into one recording. No sample was
resampled, trimmed, normalized, crossfaded or otherwise altered; the files are the upstream
bytes under their upstream names. CC BY-SA 3.0 draws this distinction itself:

> "Collection" means a collection of literary or artistic works … in which the Work in its
> entirety in unmodified form, along with one or more other contributions, constituting
> separate and independent works in themselves, are assembled into a collective whole.

and, in the definition of Adaptation, "a work that constitutes a Collection will not be
considered an Adaptation for the purpose of this License".

So the salamander files here stay under CC BY-SA 3.0 with Alexander Holm's attribution, the
smdrums files stay under the grant below, and this repository claims no licence of its own
over either. **The full CC BY-SA 3.0 text is in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE)** — it is that license,
not this repository's, and it does not reach the smdrums material.

## The smdrums grant

The SM Drums authors publish no licence document; the upstream repository has no `LICENSE`
file and none accompanies the download. The grant relied on here is the project announcement:

> ALL of the content on this website is for FREE royalty free use by anyone for anything.

Source: <https://www.kvraudio.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=6072198>
